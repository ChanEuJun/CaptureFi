CaptureFi: The Pitch Narrative & Script Strategy

The Core Hook (For Video & Presentation)
"We all doomscroll. We all see the alpha. But by the time we open an exchange, bridge funds, and calculate the hedge, the candle is gone. CaptureFi closes the gap between 'Reading' and 'Revenue'."

1. Addressing the "Narrative Trading" Gap (Pear Protocol)
The Problem: Financial alpha today is unstructured text. "Long AI, Short Legacy." "ETH is bleeding against SOL."
The Solution: CaptureFi acts as a translator. We use an LLM to parse this text and map it instantly to a Pear Protocol Pair Trade (e.g., Long SOL / Short ETH). We turn "Vibes" into "Positions".

2. The Trust Paradox (Salt)
The Problem: "I want an AI to trade for me, but I don't trust it with my private keys."
The Solution: Salt Programmable Accounts. We create a "Limited PoA" for the AI. The Salt Policy acts as a firewall: "This AI Agent can execute trades on Pear Protocol, but it CANNOT withdraw funds." It is a trustless Robo-Manager.

3. The Frictionless Fund (LI.FI)
The Problem: "I found the trade, but my money is on Optimism."
The Solution: Just-in-Time Bridging. CaptureFi detects the collateral required for the trade. If you are short, it pops up the LI.FI widget pre-filled with the exact deficit amount, bridging instantly to the trade execution address.
